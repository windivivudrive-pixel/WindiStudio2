# Windi international kits: niches and launch test

Research date: 2026-10-10. Prices below are proposed USD test prices, not sales
forecasts or market averages. “5–20 USDT” is treated as a comparable price band;
no crypto checkout or wallet was created. No products were uploaded/published.

## Evidence and its limits

Fiverr's September 13, 2026 Business Trends Index reports growing searches for
AI UGC video ads (+265%), short-form video editing (+27%), YouTube automation
and faceless-channel work (over +230%), Excel data cleaning (+210%) and PDF to
Excel (+153%). This is service-search evidence; it does not measure paid-kit
sales or prove a buyer will pay $19 for our package.
Source: https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/reports/business-trends-index-june-2026/

Upwork's February 4, 2026 report shows +329% growth in AI video generation and
editing. Its methodology uses completed US-demand jobs and freelancer earnings
in 2025 versus 2024, rather than October 2026 kit sales. This is supporting
category evidence, not proof of current storefront conversion.
Source: https://investors.upwork.com/node/12681/pdf

Start with a single $19 Flow Video Lite listing. Position product demos as the
lead use case and include faceless/SaaS content presets as bonuses. These are
three script structures sharing one visual template, not three separate apps.
Avoid listing the same ZIP under several product names. Build a genuinely
specialized next product only after observing buyer questions and paid use.

## International versus Vietnamese edition

- International SKU `windi-flow-video-lite-intl`: English source kit, brought-in
  assets and narration, own folder/skill/composition, optional manual Flow or an
  already installed bridge; no hosted voice balance, domestic personal installer,
  sample fonts or shared customer configuration.
- Vietnamese edition: retain existing full managed workflow, branding, price,
  voice/credit and support entitlements. No domestic releases/pages changed.
- Scope differentiation should explain the price difference. Do not block
  Vietnamese buyers by location or market Lite as the same full product cheaper.
- Keep the brand but describe capabilities separately. Provider names are
  integration references, not endorsements or included subscriptions.

## Store and fee implications

Whop supports software downloads and an optional license-key integration:
https://docs.whop.com/supported-business-models/saas . This release is delivered
as a ZIP and does not implement remote activation. Software checks/keys would
need a separately designed service and affect offline use.

Whop fees depend on payment method, international cards, conversion, optional
billing/tax/affiliate features and payout route:
https://whop.com/network/pricing/ . Creator payouts include Vietnam subject to
verification and available destinations:
https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/set-up-payouts .

Gumroad's published direct fee is 10% + $0.50 excluding card processing of
2.9% + $0.30. Under those card assumptions, a $5 sale leaves about $3.56 and a
$19 sale about $15.75 before taxes, refunds, currency/payout costs and affiliates.
Discover's 30% includes processing. Check actual account terms at launch:
https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees . Use $5 for an add-on with little
support, not for a full cross-platform automation product.

## Validation experiment, not a revenue promise

1. Test the package with real Windows/macOS buyers before broad platform claims.
2. Record one real product-demo preview with owned assets and an English tutorial.
3. Choose one storefront initially; share the same tracked link from a focused
   demo/tutorial. Views, qualified store visits and paid orders are different metrics.
4. Review the first 100 qualified visits and initial buyer sessions. Track conversion,
   installation failures, support minutes, refund requests and actual net proceeds.
   100 visits is a practical first review point, not statistically decisive proof.
5. Revise the onboarding/offer before adding paid ads or multiplying SKUs.
