Keep dependency license notices when redistributing any permitted component. This document is a dependency map, not a completed legal clearance for every transitive package.

Individuals and qualifying small organizations may be eligible for free use; others need the appropriate company license. Do not resell Remotion itself or represent a Windi purchase as a Remotion license. Review the terms applicable to this pinned version and your use: https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/blob/v4.0.520/LICENSE.md and https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq .
- **React / React DOM:** MIT, Meta Platforms and contributors. https://github.com/facebook/react/blob/main/LICENSE .
- **TypeScript:** Apache-2.0, Microsoft and contributors. https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript/blob/main/LICENSE.txt .
- **@types/react and transitives:** consult their installed package LICENSE files. Inspect the lockfile before release. No upstream source/license notices are replaced by the Windi branding.
- **Renderer browser / codecs:** installed by Remotion where required; their own terms apply. No browser or FFmpeg binary is included in this ZIP.
- **Flow and AI agents:** optional external services with their own account, region, subscription and usage terms. The kit supplies neither access nor quota and makes no promise of an officially supported subscription bridge. Existing Windi Connect use is separate and needs its own provenance/terms review before bundling; it is not bundled here.

Only system fonts are referenced. No SVN Calling Code font, sample Windi voice, domestic account configuration, video recording, music, third-party skill or generated marketing asset is included.
