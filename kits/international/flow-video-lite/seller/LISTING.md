## Title

Windi Flow Video Lite — Short Product Demos & Explainers with Your AI Agent

## Short description

Turn your own images, clips and narration into an editable short-video project.
Includes English AI-agent instructions, three content presets, local preview and
MP4 export. Bring your own media and AI subscriptions. Source kit, not a desktop app.

## What you get

- Three editable content presets: product ad, faceless explainer and SaaS demo.
- One visual template in portrait and square formats, with brand colors,
  headlines, scene captions, image motion and local video playback.
- Environment checks, project initialization and a uniquely named agent skill.
- Local Remotion preview and H.264 MP4 export, plus scene-level SRT.
- Source files, buyer license and third-party dependency notes.

## Before buying

This beta requires Node.js 20+, an AI coding agent or willingness to edit JSON,
and internet for initial dependencies/browser downloads. The native acceptance
status is listed in QA.md. There are no bundled AI credits, LLM accounts, voices,
browser extensions or stock media. Optional Flow use requires your own eligible
account. No automatic word timing, dubbing or social publishing is included.
Commercial rendered videos are permitted by the Windi buyer license; Remotion
and asset licenses apply separately. No resale rights to the kit are included.
